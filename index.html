About Wudassie Diagnostic Center

Established 2008 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Who We Are

Founded in 2008 by Dawit Hailu and his family, Wudassie Diagnostic Center (WDC) is one of the first diagnostic centers with MRI, CT scan, Digital X-ray, Ultrasound, EKG, and Ambulance service in Ethiopia.

WDC's passion for quality, cost-effectiveness, and exceptional patient care were contagious and continue to be our core principles of operation. WDC is dedicated to providing the community in and outside Addis Ababa with the finest imaging services by a local team of specialized physicians and associates. This ensures that our patients receive care from a team who works closely with the local healthcare community, including the referring physicians.

In addition, the WDC team is connected with other physicians and professionals across the country via WDC's network, giving the Center the ability to share best practices and consult with other experts on special cases.

Medical Capabilities

Our 7 Diagnostic Services

High-precision medical imaging & clinical diagnostic services.

1. Ambulance Services

24/7 patient transport and emergency transfer equipped with trained medical personnel to ensure rapid and safe transit for diagnostic testing.

2. CT-Scan

High-speed multi-slice Computed Tomography for rapid trauma assessment, head, chest, abdominal, and vascular cross-sectional imaging with low radiation exposure.

3. Endoscopy and Colonoscopy

Gastrointestinal internal examinations providing direct video visualization of the digestive tract for detection and evaluation of ulcers, bleeding, and mucosal lesions.

4. Laboratory

Wudassie Advanced Diagnostic Laboratory offers comprehensive chemistry, hematology, urinalysis, microbiology, hormone profiling, ELISA, and PCR testing.

5. MRI

High-field Magnetic Resonance Imaging delivering detailed neuro-radiology, brain, spinal, musculoskeletal, and soft tissue scans with zero ionizing radiation.

6. Radiography

First center to introduce high-resolution Digital X-Ray services in Ethiopia for fast orthopedic, chest, abdominal, and specialized contrast examinations.

7. Ultrasonography

Advanced 3D/4D ultrasound imaging and Doppler ultrasound studies for obstetric, abdominal, vascular, thyroid, and pelvic diagnostic evaluations.
